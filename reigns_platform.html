Who We Are

Not an NGO. A professional, fee-earning development partner.

REIGNS is a Ghana-based development consultancy built to turn evidence, capability, and innovation into measurable development outcomes. We work at the point where research meets implementation — generating credible evidence, strengthening people and institutions, designing practical solutions, and building delivery systems that keep creating value long after a project cycle ends.

We are commissioned by governments, development partners, private organizations, academic institutions, and civil society actors when the work in front of them has to be rigorous, practical, and accountable.

Our Unique Philosophy

The Regenerative Development Approach

Development investments should not become obsolete when a project closes. REIGNS works with a regenerative mindset: understand what has been created, identify what remains unfinished, and convert existing assets into new pathways for impact.

The Market Distinction: This is the one idea in the REIGNS model no other firm in the market is currently running.
01 Recycle

Identify underused knowledge, relationships, infrastructure, skills, data, and community assets created through previous interventions.

02 Reframe

Work collaboratively with stakeholders to reinterpret persistent problems and co-create practical new pathways.

03 Renew

Use evidence, learning, and diagnostics to improve, adapt, and extend what already exists rather than starting from zero.

04 Redefine

Look beyond income alone to opportunity, access, skills, dignity, agency, and connections that shape people's ability to thrive.
